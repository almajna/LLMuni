        Method

        1. Real city, real week

          Muni and BART timetables (GTFS) for a fixed week, and San Francisco's stores from OpenStreetMap with parseable opening hours.

        2. Door to door

          R5 routes every trip on foot, bus, rail and BART, on a five-minute departure grid, so every plan and the optimum share one clock.

        3. Errand days

          Three hundred seeded tasks in three tiers: stores to visit, closing times, deadlines and a meet-up. One in ten is impossible by design.

        4. Provable optimum

          An exact search over errands done and places reached finds the fastest plan; brute force and an integer program agree with it.

        5. Replay

          Each answer is matched to real stores and replayed on the timetable. Stores missing from OpenStreetMap are checked against the city's business registry.

        Limits

        • Scheduled timetables, not real-time: no delays are modelled.
        • OpenStreetMap hours can be incomplete or out of date; tasks only use stores with parseable hours.
        • Walking speed (1.3 m/s) and time spent in each store are assumptions.
        • Each model answers each task once, at its default temperature, with medium reasoning effort.
